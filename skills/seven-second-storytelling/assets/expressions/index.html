ABOUT MUSE

The face carries
the gesture.

One original adult woman, sculpted in Three.js. Eight five-second studies coordinate the eyebrows, eyelids, cheeks, lips and jaw. Pause any moment and orbit the portrait to inspect its form.

These are authored acting studies. The expression names describe the performance; they do not diagnose emotion or reveal a person’s inner state.

FORM remains the separate atlas for body, arm, hand, leg and foot. The native SVG collection is preserved alongside both editions.
