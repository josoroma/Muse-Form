ABOUT FORM

Move it.
Understand its shape.

Five original Three.js studies connect anatomical form with a visible action. Orbit any model, pause a five-second loop, and select a structure to follow it in space.

One continuous character connects all five studies and the MUSE portrait. The surface regions simplify anatomy for visual explanation. Joint angles describe these authored poses; surface highlights identify structures, rather than measured muscle activation.

The line hierarchy grows from the Hairline-inspired SVG collection. The skinned model, motion and annotations remain editable.
